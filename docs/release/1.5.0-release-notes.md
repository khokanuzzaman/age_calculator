# Age Calculator 1.5.0 Release Notes

## Google Play

Explore your day with the Today Dashboard, organize birthdays in My People,
and set smart birthday reminders. Create and share Birthday Wishes, track
recurring Life Timeline anniversaries, and use the expanded Date Tools. Includes
reliability improvements.

## App Store

Meet the Today Dashboard and My People birthday tracker with smart reminders.
Create and share Birthday Wishes, follow recurring Life Timeline anniversaries,
and use more Date Tools. Includes reliability improvements.

## Internal summary

- Added a Primary Profile and personalized Today Dashboard while keeping generic
  age calculations independent of the saved profile.
- Expanded My People with favorites, per-person reminder preferences, birthday
  cards, and shareable wishes.
- Added Life Timeline categories, recurring anniversaries, reminder settings,
  and Life Calendar integration.
- Added Date Tools for date arithmetic, working days, weekdays, DOB finding,
  countdowns, and leap-year checks.
- Added privacy-conscious product analytics, Crashlytics fatal-error handling,
  a conservative native review flow, and release resilience around optional
  services.
